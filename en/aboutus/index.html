Global Reach and Innovation in Hoist and Crane Solutions
#

LET WE BEAR YOUR LOAD
#

Since 1972, Cheng Day International Group has established itself as a leading manufacturer in the hoist and crane industry. The company is organized into four main business departments: Crane & Hoist, Elevator, Parking System, and Heavy Duty. Our expertise spans marketing, product design, manufacturing, testing, installation, and after-sales service.

Currently, Cheng Day operates three factories located in Taiwan and China: Cheng Day Taiwan (Headquarters), Grand Asia Machinery Industry (Kunshan) Co., Ltd., and Kunshan Ching-Tu Elevator Co., Ltd. We also maintain overseas branches, including Black Bear Corp. in the USA and a branch in Osaka, Japan. Our global sales network covers over 70 countries, with professionals across Asia, the US, Europe, Africa, and Oceania, all contributing to the sustainable growth of the Cheng Day Group.

We offer our own brands—“Black Bear”, “U-Mega”, and “Yong Sheng”—for standard products, and we specialize in custom-made solutions. In addition, we provide OEM and ODM services for renowned customers worldwide.

Commitment to Quality and Manufacturing Excellence
#

Our components are crafted from premium raw materials and high-strength alloys. Advanced manufacturing technologies—including CNC horizontal machining centers, robotic welders, precise scanning probes, and automated motor test systems—ensure consistent, high-quality production. Each hoist undergoes individual testing and inspection using automated systems and stringent quality control standards.

Cheng Day holds SGS ISO 9001:2015 and CE certifications, as well as domestic crane and hoist certifications. We have received the 8th Taiwan National Foundation Award and the National Industrial Innovation Award for our Green Power motor. Our products are protected by patents in Germany, the USA, Japan, and Taiwan, underscoring our commitment to international standards and ongoing innovation in lifting equipment.

Embracing Innovation and Speed in a Changing Market
#

In today’s rapidly evolving market, success hinges on innovation and agility. The traditional mindset of “to be the same should be million” is being replaced by a focus on differentiation and responsiveness to market demands. At Cheng Day, we integrate market insights and innovative thinking, always prioritizing the customer’s perspective and adapting our commercial strategies to create successful, enduring products.

Our culture emphasizes speed and efficiency, striving to meet and exceed customer expectations. By understanding the unique needs of different markets and leveraging the expertise of our management and team, we are able to develop and deliver valuable products quickly and effectively. Our mission is to foster innovation, collaboration, and mutual growth with our supply chain and sales partners, deepening our impact across diverse industries and cultures.
